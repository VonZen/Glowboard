Good to know.

Where can I get Glowboard?
Find Glowboard on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
Do I need an account?
No Glowboard account is needed. Make a banner, choose a look and hold it up. Your creations are saved on your device.
What’s free, and what’s Pro?
Core banner styles, text colours and basic playback are free. Pro adds advanced customisation, sound-reactive features, imported music and video or GIF export.
Does the website save my message?
No. This preview runs in your browser and doesn’t upload or save the text you type. The Privacy policy explains the app’s separate usage analytics and purchase services.
